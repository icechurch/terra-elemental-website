Who we are

Soil biotechnology for the land Coloradans care for

Terra Elemental provides access to regenerative, sustainable and holistic biotechnology that serves the needs of soil, plants and people. We work with parks and recreation departments, grounds managers, golf courses, growers and restoration teams across Southern Colorado.

Through consulting and supply, we help clients replace costly conventional amendments with effective, affordable biological programs that rebuild soil from the ground up, so it holds water, cycles nutrients and supports healthy plants year after year.

How it works

Living soil technology

Our inoculated granules work as a complete soil support system, combining time-release organic nutrients, mineral inputs and a dense, active microbial community.

  1. 1

    Nutrient foundation

    Derived from agricultural byproducts, the organic base provides dense, time-release nutrition for plants and microbes.

  2. 2

    Mineral enrichment

    Potassium sulfate and other minerals supply essential macro- and micronutrients.

  3. 3

    Microbial activation

    Granules are inoculated with beneficial bacteria and fungi that activate when watered in.

  4. 4

    Living soil

    Microbes distribute nutrients, retain water and turn soil into a thriving ecosystem.

Our manufacturing partner

Proven products from Sigma AgriScience

Terra Elemental is an authorized distributor of Sigma AgriScience, a leading North American manufacturer of organic granular biofertilizers since 2007. Sigma supplies agriculture, nursery, turf and landscape markets across the U.S. and internationally.

Sigma's biotechnology is harnessed from nature and never genetically modified. Its proprietary GranCORE process combines humic acid and essential nutrients into a slow-release, easy-to-spread granule infused with beneficial biology.
